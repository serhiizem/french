# 05 · Vocabulaire — Relations et communication

Learn each verb **with its construction**: *qqn* (COD) or *à qqn* (COI).

## Verbes : COD (verb + qqn)

| Français | English |
|---|---|
| aider qqn | to help someone |
| écouter qqn | to listen to someone |
| attendre qqn | to wait for someone |
| chercher qqn | to look for someone |
| appeler qqn | to call someone |
| remercier qqn | to thank someone |
| inviter qqn | to invite someone |
| accompagner qqn | to go with someone |

## Verbes : COI (verb + à qqn)

| Français | English |
|---|---|
| téléphoner à qqn | to phone someone |
| demander à qqn | to ask someone |
| répondre à qqn | to answer someone |
| ressembler à qqn | to look like someone |
| sourire à qqn | to smile at someone |
| conseiller à qqn | to advise someone |
| manquer à qqn | to be missed by someone |
